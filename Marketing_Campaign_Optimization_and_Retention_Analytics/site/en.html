01 · OVERVIEW

Four layers, one evidence-oriented decision

The project starts by describing the funnel and channels, tests conversion differences, estimates economic value under explicit assumptions and uses a retention model to prioritise actions. The sequence is useful as a decision structure; it does not turn simulation into causal impact.

01 · DescriptiveFunnel, channels, RFM-proxy segments and language personalisation.
02 · InferentialZ-test, confidence interval and Monte Carlo power analysis.
03 · PredictiveRetention experiments and false-positive diagnosis.
04 · PrescriptiveExpected value, incremental profit and recommended action.

Business question

Which channel, message and user group deserve investment when conversion, retention and economic value are not the same thing?

Project answer

Combine conversion, retention and segment-LTV signals into a campaign recommendation, with costs and revenue treated as configurable parameters.

Main reading: the chain is modular and operationally legible, but any campaign policy needs real costs, clear attribution and controlled experimentation.

03 · EXPERIMENT AND INFERENCE

Personalisation showed no uplift in the canonical cohort

In the stable-user base, conversion was 10.29% in control and 10.24% in personalisation: a −0.06 percentage-point difference, z = 0.0779, p = 0.937870 and 95% CI [−1.50; 1.38] p.p.

10.24%personalisation conversion
−0.06 p.p.observed absolute difference
−0.56%observed relative uplift

Planning

Power analysis defines a 2 p.p. minimum lift, alpha 0.05, target power 0.8 and 3,916 users per group. Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 experiments estimated 80.0% power.

Sample sufficiency

The canonical base contains 3,225 control users and 3,624 personalisation users. Both groups are below the 3,916 requirement; the executed test remains exploratory and underpowered for the planned lift.

Not established: there is no evidence of uplift in this cohort, and the result does not claim causality without confirmed randomisation and a prospective experiment with adequate sample size.

Interpretation: the model is predictive and exploratory. The not-retained class contains 246 users in the canonical base; any preventive campaign requires a dedicated A/B validation before scaling.

Operational signal

The two false positives were inspected by channel, variant, age and language. The diagnosis is exploratory and subject to the low error count.

Responsible interpretation

These patterns guide error investigation and monitoring; they do not prove causal bias or authorise automated decisions about groups.

06 · PRESCRIPTION AND GOVERNANCE

Recommendations as decision support, not automatic decisions

The expected-value function combines retention probability, segment LTV and per-user cost. It generates loyalty, promotion, win-back or no-action recommendations. The notebook also exports an individual CRM file, but this report neither publishes nor links to it because it contains user_id and row-level recommendations.

It can support

  • A complete analytics chain from funnel to recommendation.
  • The A/B unit is explicitly a stable user, with 460 variant switchers excluded.
  • PT-BR and EN-US now share the same published predictive scenario.
  • Financial assumptions are explicit in code and can be recalculated.

It cannot support

  • That personalisation caused uplift; the test found no difference and was below the planned sample size.
  • That simulated ROI represents realised profit.
  • That individual recommendations are production-ready or safe to share publicly.
  • That retention prediction replaces a controlled experiment.

Use decision: portfolio and investigation artefact. Validate real costs, complete the planned sample and test policies with experimental control before automating campaigns.
