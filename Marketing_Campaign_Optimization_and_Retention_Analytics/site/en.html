01 · OVERVIEW

Four layers, one evidence-oriented decision

The project starts by describing the funnel and channels, tests conversion differences, estimates economic value under explicit assumptions and uses a retention model to prioritise actions. The sequence is useful as a decision structure; it does not turn simulation into causal impact.

01 · DescriptiveFunnel, channels, RFM-proxy segments and language personalisation.
02 · InferentialZ-test, confidence interval and Monte Carlo power analysis.
03 · PredictiveRetention experiments and false-positive diagnosis.
04 · PrescriptiveExpected value, incremental profit and recommended action.

Business question

Which channel, message and user group deserve investment when conversion, retention and economic value are not the same thing?

Project answer

Combine conversion, retention and segment-LTV signals into a campaign recommendation, with costs and revenue treated as configurable parameters.

Main reading: the chain is modular and operationally legible, but any campaign policy needs real costs, clear attribution and controlled experimentation.

03 · EXPERIMENT AND INFERENCE

Personalisation is associated with higher conversion

The proportions test compares control and personalisation. In the executed output, conversion was 7.34% in control and 14.24% in personalisation: a 6.90 percentage-point difference, z = −10.9983, p < 0.000001 and a 95% CI of [5.67; 8.12] p.p.

7.34%control conversion
14.24%personalisation conversion
+6.90 p.p.observed absolute difference
93.99%observed relative uplift

Planning

Power analysis defines a 2 p.p. minimum lift, alpha 0.05, target power 0.8 and 4,063 users per group. Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 experiments estimated 79.2% power.

Interpretation caution

The notebook contains different sample-sufficiency readings: one compares 7,309 users with 8,126 required; another records 4,825 as the smallest group and proceeds. The denominator must be reconciled before operational use.

Not established: the result is statistically significant in the analysed dataset, but this page does not claim causal uplift without confirmation of randomisation, the eligible denominator and prospective experiment execution.

05 · RETENTION AND ERRORS

The model prioritises risk, but the notebooks are not identical

Scenarios were compared with and without conversion as a feature and with different balancing strategies. The best scenario depends on the notebook and the dependencies available in its environment.

Recorded divergence: the EN-US edition executed optional models that were unavailable in the PT-BR output, and false-negative metrics also vary. Before production, fix the environment, seed, feature set and selection rule in one canonical run.

Operational signal

False positives were analysed by channel, variant, age and language. House Ads and the control variant appear over-represented among false positives, but this is exploratory evidence.

Responsible interpretation

These patterns guide error investigation and monitoring; they do not prove causal bias or authorise automated decisions about groups.

06 · PRESCRIPTION AND GOVERNANCE

Recommendations as decision support, not automatic decisions

The expected-value function combines retention probability, segment LTV and per-user cost. It generates actions such as loyalty, promotion, win-back or no action. The notebook also exports an individual CRM file, but this report neither publishes nor links to it because it contains user_id and row-level recommendations.

It can support

  • A complete analytics chain from funnel to recommendation.
  • Separate layers for conversion, retention and economic efficiency.
  • Operational error inspection by campaign and user attributes.
  • Recalculation of financial assumptions through explicit code constants.

It cannot support

  • That the observed uplift is causal without a confirmed experimental design.
  • That simulated ROI represents realised profit.
  • That PT and EN differences are only rounding.
  • That individual recommendations are production-ready or safe to share publicly.

Use decision: portfolio and investigation artefact. Fix the canonical run, reconcile power-analysis denominators, validate real costs and test policies with experimental control before automating campaigns.
