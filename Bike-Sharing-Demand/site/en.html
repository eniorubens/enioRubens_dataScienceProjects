01 · OVERVIEW

A normal-operations forecast that preserves the 2020 regime shift

The project estimates hourly rentals from mobility history and meteorological variables. An extraordinary 2020 mobility collapse could not be explained by the available sensors; the year was therefore excluded from fitting and the primary metric, while remaining visible as a stress diagnostic.

01 · Explore2015–2024 EDA, seasonality, weather, and operating regimes.
02 · BuildTemporal and weather features inside dynamic pipelines.
03 · ConfirmTemporal selection, sealed holdout, and a pre-registered Champion.
04 · SupportConformal intervals and a capacity-decision replay.

Main reading: CatBoost was confirmed as the holdout Champion; U4b improves average interval coverage but remains experimental because of conditional-coverage alerts.

03 · EXPERIMENTAL UNCERTAINTY

Calibrating the interval does not change the point forecast

Favorable evidence

Across the four selection folds, coverage ranged from 90.024% to 90.242%. A 500-repetition temporal bootstrap produced an interval from 89.709% to 90.591%, including the nominal target.

Preserved limit

Alerts remain for morning rush, evening rush, very high demand, and Fridays at 6 p.m. U4b is an experimental candidate, not a production-ready component.

Interpretation: the gain is restricted to prediction intervals; E0 MAE, RMSE, and R² were unchanged.

05 · REUSABLE ARCHITECTURE

The notebook explains logic that lives in src/

The project preserves code reuse: data access, features, temporal folds, pipelines, tracking, validation, uncertainty, and reports live in testable modules. The eight PT-BR and EN-US notebooks expose the same analysis with localized narrative.

Components

  • src/data.py, feature_engineering.py, and periodic_features.py
  • src/cv.py, temporal_optimizer.py, and modeling_pipeline.py
  • src/final_validation.py and conformal_uncertainty.py
  • tests/ for leakage, splits, contracts, reports, and notebooks

Analytical order

  • 01–03: EDA, multivariate analysis, and features
  • 04: model selection and candidate freezing
  • 05: final validation on the sealed holdout
  • 06–08: uncertainty experiments, calibration, and replay

Frozen manifests record dataset, candidate, holdout, environment, and artifact fingerprints. This page summarizes results; notebooks and modules remain the executable source of truth.

06 · GOVERNANCE AND LIMITATIONS

What the project can and cannot support

It can support

  • Auditable temporal comparison under normal operations.
  • A CatBoost Champion confirmed on a twelve-month holdout.
  • Experimental conformal intervals with average coverage near 90%.
  • An explicit demonstration of a forecast-and-uncertainty capacity decision.

It cannot support

  • Generalization to a new temporal window without independent confirmation.
  • Adequate conditional coverage in every demand regime.
  • Production readiness of U4b without monitoring and online evaluation.
  • That the operational demonstration is a prospective test.

Use decision: portfolio and investigation artifact. Before operational use, jointly freeze E0/E4/U4b, validate a new temporal window, monitor drift, and address regime-specific alerts.
