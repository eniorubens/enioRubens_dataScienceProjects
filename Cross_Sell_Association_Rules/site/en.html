Popularity is not association.

01 · OVERVIEW

One question, one important distinction

Popular items appear in many baskets, but a useful association requires comparing observed co-occurrence with what would be expected from marginal frequencies. The notebook follows the full chain: transaction aggregation, one-hot matrix, Apriori, rule generation, metrics and visualisations.

01 · PrepareCustomer × date becomes an item basket.
02 · MeasureSupport, confidence, lift, conviction and Zhang.
03 · MineApriori with itemset and rule thresholds.
04 · PrioritiseLift and Zhang filters for candidates.

Business question

Which product pairs deserve a bundle, placement or recommendation test?

Project answer

Do not use popularity rankings alone; prioritise co-occurrences above the independence expectation.

Main reading: the project provides quantitative association triage, not proof that a recommendation will cause a purchase.

03 · EVIDENCE AGAINST POPULARITY

The two most frequent items do not form a good rule

Whole milk and other vegetables are the most popular items, with individual support of 0.1579 and 0.1221. Together, they appear in only 0.0148 of baskets.

0.1579whole milk support
0.1221other vegetables support
0.77pair lift
0.97pair conviction

Rule reading

Confidence is 0.09 for whole milk → other vegetables and 0.12 in the reverse direction. Lift below 1 indicates lower co-occurrence than expected under independence.

Complementary metric

Zhang = −0.2625. Lift, conviction and Zhang converge: item popularity is not enough evidence of a favourable association.

Decision: discard this pair as a priority candidate when the criterion is association above expectation, despite its high frequency.

06 · GOVERNANCE AND NEXT TEST

Association is triage, not causality

The rules describe historical patterns in aggregated baskets. They do not show that displaying one product will make a customer buy another, nor estimate profit without price, margin, placement, inventory and policy experimentation.

It can support

  • Purchase frequency and association strength are different dimensions.
  • The most popular pair analysed has lift below 1.
  • Lift and Zhang filters produce interpretable candidates.
  • PT-BR and EN-US use the same dataset, parameters and numerical results.

It cannot support

  • That a rule causes increased sales.
  • That candidates maximise margin or revenue.
  • That the ranking remains stable in another time window.
  • That a recommendation is production-ready without controlled testing.

Next step: test a small set of rules in a controlled experiment, measuring incremental conversion, margin, inventory and segment effects.
